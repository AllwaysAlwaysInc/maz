# Maz — MandalOz 3D & CROW Chain RPG

A 3D Over-the-Shoulder Dungeon Crawler, Collectible Card RPG, and CROW Chain Wallet powered by Three.js and custom Meshy 3D figurines.

## Quick Start

1. Double-click `Start_Maz_Game.bat` or run:
   ```bash
   python server.py
   ```
2. Open **http://127.0.0.1:8910/mandaloz.html** to play **MandalOz 3D**, or **http://127.0.0.1:8910/** for the **Maz Wallet Hub**.

## Features
- **Over-the-Shoulder 3D Labyrinth**: Procedural elemental mazes (`Grass`, `Fire`, `Water`, `Stone`, `Electric`) across **Easy (5×5)**, **Hard (7×7)**, **Legendary (9×9 • 2F)**, and **Nightmare (11×11 • 3F)**.
- **Fog-of-War & 🦇 Bat Pulse Radar**: Lazy 3D rendering revealed as you explore or cast Maz Stinky Bat sonar pulses.
- **Turn-Based AP Party Combat**: Solo or up to 3 party members (scales to 4 at Lv.20 and 5 at Lv.40), with Equipped Card AP/Crit/HP buffs and Hardcore Trial Permadeath.
- **🧙 Hero, Gear & 3D + 60-Figurine Vault**: Pick your 1st starter figurine (including Maz), roll new figurines with Ring Shards, and equip collectible RPG cards into 10 dedicated slots.
- **🎴 5-Card Gacha Booster Packs & ⛩️ MandalOz Shrine**: Pull cards, fuse duplicates in the Fusion Lab, and mint assets on CROW Chain.
